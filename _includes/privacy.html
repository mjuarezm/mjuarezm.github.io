Alfred Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alfred ("Alfred", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Alfred application and connect it to your Google account. By using Alfred you agree to the practices described here.

1. What Alfred does

Alfred is an assistant that connects to your Google account and uses Google APIs to read and interact with your Google apps, such as Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Sheets, in order to carry out the tasks you request.

2. Information we access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, Alfred may access the following, limited to the permissions (scopes) you approve:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Gmail data: email messages, labels, and metadata.
  • Google Drive data: file names, metadata, and file contents.
  • Google Sheets data: spreadsheet contents and structure.
  • Other Google apps data: data from any other Google service you choose to connect, as shown on the Google consent screen.

We also store the OAuth tokens Google issues so Alfred can act on your behalf until you revoke access.

3. How we use your information

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of Alfred that you request, such as reading, searching, summarizing, and acting on your emails, files, and spreadsheets. We do not use your Google data for any other purpose.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy – Limited Use

Alfred's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide or improve user-facing features that are visible in Alfred's interface.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide or improve those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data unless we have your affirmative consent for specific messages or files, it is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), it is necessary to comply with applicable law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and protecting stored credentials and tokens against unauthorized access. We retain Google user data only as long as needed to provide the features you request, and we do not keep it longer than necessary. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only with service providers that help us operate Alfred (for example, hosting providers) and only to the extent needed to deliver the service, or where required by law. Any such providers are bound to handle data consistent with this policy and the Limited Use requirements above.

7. Your choices and control

  • Revoke access: you can remove Alfred's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your data: email us at the address below and we will delete the data we hold about you, including stored tokens, within a reasonable time (generally within 30 days).
  • Access or correction: you may ask us what information we hold about you, and request corrections.

8. Children's privacy

Alfred is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated through the app or by email where appropriate.
